Circular participant avatar used everywhere a character appears: chat rows
(24px), the players panel (32px), cinematic plates (44px), and cards (larger).
With `avatarUrl` it shows the themed portrait (fading in over the gradient);
without it, a deterministic per-name gradient circle with the initial letter.
`isGM` adds the Game Master ring; `isDead` applies the grayscale/cross
treatment.
