Role explainer modal, styled after CharacterCard: chalk-on-blackboard role art,
role tags (plus YOUR ROLE when `own`), night-order line, ability text, and a
"Got it" button. Fullscreen fixed overlay — pass `imageSrc` in designs (the
app's static `/roles/<role>.jpg` asset is not reachable from a design) and wrap
in a sized, `transform`ed container. Roles: werewolf, doctor, detective,
maniac, villager.
