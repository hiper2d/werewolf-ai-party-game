**Update: reframe any portrait** 🖼️

Every cast is drawn on one sheet, and the game cuts each character out of it. When a cut lands badly (half a forehead, a neighbour's shoulder), you can now fix it yourself:

• open a portrait and **move or resize the card frame** on the drawn sheet
• place the **round avatar** inside the card
• save. Nothing is redrawn, nothing is charged, and the new crop shows up everywhere the character appears

Works on the new-game preview and on character cards during a game. The preset mannequins got their own sheet, so they can be reframed too.

Also new: the **Create Game page was redesigned**, with grouped settings and a compact cast list, and the cutter now reads the sheet's actual grid lines instead of assuming equal cells, so fresh draws land better out of the box.

Portraits drawn before this update keep their original crop. Redraw the cast to get the new editor on an older game.
